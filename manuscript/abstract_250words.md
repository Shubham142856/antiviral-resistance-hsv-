# Candidate Journal Abstract: Position-Grouped Cross-Validation Leaves a Sequence-Neighbour Shortcut Open

Machine learning models for viral drug resistance frequently evaluate performance via position-grouped cross-validation. Here, we introduce a label-free Pre-Hoc Split Separation Diagnostic measuring sequence-neighbour distance ($\min |p_{\text{test}} - p_{\text{train}}|$) prior to model fitting, showing that position-grouped holdouts fail to close sequence correlation channels: on HSV-1 thymidine kinase (UL23), 96.5% of test positions sit within $\le 2$ residues of a training position (median: 1.0 aa); on DNA polymerase (UL30), 60.7–63.1% sit within $\le 2$ residues (median: 2.0 aa). Consequently, a baseline Random Forest trained solely on sequence position index achieves an apparent AUROC of 0.8715 under position-grouped splitting on UL30-aciclovir, but collapses to chance (0.5001 $\pm$ 0.0009) under domain-isolating contiguous-block holdout (median distance 22–81 aa; single-pass collapse $0.8797 \to 0.4591$, $\Delta = +0.4206$, $p < 0.0005$). Across 929 curated missense records (630 substitutions; SHA-256 `7C4D...`), leakage localizes strictly to feature representation: positional features degrade by $\Delta = 0.2336$–$0.4206$ across 6/6 combinations ($p < 0.0005$), whereas variant-level descriptors degrade substantially less (conservation 3- to 11-fold, $\Delta = 0.0362$–$0.1092$; language models 7- to 68-fold, $\Delta = 0.0062$–$0.0356$). Under our Evidence Ladder, classical multi-herpesvirus PSSM is the single baseline whose 95% bootstrap confidence interval strictly excludes 0.50 across all three targets under within-study ascertainment control ($S_0 = 0.6994$–$0.7744$). In cross-herpesvirus replication on Cytomegalovirus (HCMV UL54), a pre-specified post-hoc protocol revealed an Exonuclease Double Dissociation Crossover: distance to the proofreading pocket recovered predictive power for chain terminators (ganciclovir $S_0 = 0.7243$; cidofovir $S_0 = 0.9264$) while inverting on pyrophosphate inhibitor foscarnet ($S_0 = 0.3197$; crossover $p < 0.0005$; leave-site-out shifts $\le 0.045$). In clinical UL23 under aciclovir, truncating variants account for 38.4% (98/255) of resistance entries, isolating the non-trivial missense challenge.
